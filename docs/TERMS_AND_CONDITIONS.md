## Preamble: Community Identity & Purpose

Welcome to the **Team Taraba River Community Portal** — the official digital community platform of **Team Taraba River**, operating under the auspices of **URIP (Usosans Resident in Port Harcourt)**, a recognized regional chapter of the **Unity Schools Old Students Association (USOSA)**.

USOSA is the national umbrella body representing alumni of all 115 Federal Unity Colleges across Nigeria. Founded to champion national integration, moral excellence, and detribalized citizenship, the Unity Schools tradition is the bedrock of our fellowship. Team Taraba River brings this spirit to Port Harcourt and Rivers State through fellowship, professional networking, sports and social outings, mutual welfare assistance, and community service.

This portal is owned and operated by Team Taraba River. Access to and use of this portal is governed strictly by this Master Member Agreement (encompassing our Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Media License, and Code of Conduct).

> **Plain Language Summary:** This platform is an exclusive, secure space for verified members and alumni of Team Taraba River. By using this portal, you agree to treat everyone with dignity, protect fellow members' confidential information, uphold the highest standards of integrity, and adhere to Nigerian law.

## 1. Eligibility, Registration & Verification

1. **Eligibility Criteria:** Access to member-restricted directories, voting, and interactive portal tools is open exclusively to verified alumni of Federal Unity Colleges resident in Port Harcourt and registered members of Team Taraba River.
2. **Minimum Age Requirement:** You must be at least **18 years of age** to register an account. By registering, you warrant that you are of legal age under Nigerian law.
3. **Accuracy of Information:** Members must provide truthful, current, and complete information during registration (including Full Legal Name, School, Graduation Year, Contact Telephone, Valid Email Address, and Next of Kin). Willful falsification of alumni credentials constitutes a breach of these Terms and the Cybercrimes Act 2015.
4. **Administrative Profile Moderation:** To safeguard community standards and visual authenticity, all uploaded profile photographs and submitted media undergo administrative review before public activation. Photographs deemed inappropriate, vulgar, or misleading will be rejected.

## 2. Code of Conduct & USOSA Values

1. **USOSA Heritage Values:** Every member shall conduct themselves in a manner reflecting the core values of USOSA: unity, mutual respect, zero tolerance for tribal, religious, gender, or social discrimination, and active commitment to community welfare.
2. **Prohibited Activities:** The following behaviors are strictly prohibited on the portal and at all affiliated physical gatherings:
   - Harassment, cyberstalking, intimidation, bullying, or threatening any member.
   - Dissemination of defamatory, obscene, pornographic, sexually suggestive, or politically inciting material.
   - Impersonation of any fellow member, chapter executive, or platform administrator.
   - Unauthorized web scraping, automated data harvesting, or extraction of member phone numbers and email directories.
   - Commercial spamming, unsolicited direct messaging, financial solicitations, or fraudulent schemes.
   - Doxing or publishing any member's private residential, employment, or family data without explicit written consent.
   - Interfering with or compromising the digital infrastructure, security tokens, or database integrity of the portal.
3. **Disciplinary Remedies & Appeals Process:**
   - Platform administrators may issue formal warnings, content removals, temporary feature restrictions, or account suspensions for infractions.
   - A member subject to administrative sanctions has the right to appeal in writing to the Team Lead within **14 calendar days** of the notice.
   - Appeals are adjudicated by an independent review panel comprising at least two executive administrators not involved in the initial determination. A binding written outcome will be delivered within **7 business days**.

## 5. Privacy Policy & Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 Compliance

1. **Designated Data Controller:** Team Taraba River acts as the Data Controller under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023**.
2. **Categories of Personal Data Processed:**
   - **Public Directory Data (Members Only):** Full Name, Title, Unity School, Graduation Year, Occupation, Professional Skills, and Profile Image.
   - **Restricted Administrative Records:** Phone Number, Email Address, Residential Area, Next of Kin, and Birthday (Day & Month only).
3. **Automated Birthday Reminder Processing:**
   - The platform utilizes an automated background scheduling engine that runs daily at **12:00 PM WAT**.
   - The engine processes birthday records (**Day and Month only — birth years are never collected, stored, or displayed**) to generate advance monthly planning digests and daily 24-hour eve reminders.
   - Notices are dispatched securely via transactional email services (Resend) to official team administrators (`tarabateam@gmail.com`) strictly for community felicitations and WhatsApp announcements.
4. **Zero-Sale & Zero-Monetization Guarantee:** Personal data is **never sold, leased, rented, or shared** with commercial third-party advertisers, brokers, or external entities.
5. **Cross-Border Cloud Infrastructure:** In compliance with Sections 41–43 of the NDPA 2023, data stored on Google Cloud Firebase and associated cloud backends is safeguarded by enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit within SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified data centers.
6. **Data Subject Statutory Rights:** Under the NDPA 2023, members have the right to:
   - Access their complete stored personal data file.
   - Request immediate rectification of inaccurate or outdated details.
   - Request permanent deletion of their profile and account ("Right to Erasure"), executed within **30 calendar days**.
   - Request data portability in a structured, machine-readable format.
   - Lodge a formal inquiry with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** if they believe their data rights have been infringed.
7. **Security Incident & Breach Notification SLA:** In the unlikely event of a verified data breach affecting personal records, Team Taraba River will notify affected members and the NDPC within **72 hours** of formal confirmation, as mandated by the NDPA 2023.

## 6. Local Storage, Cookies & Analytics Transparency

1. **No Invasive Third-Party Tracking:** The portal does **not** employ third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracker pixels, or biometric profiling beacons.
